PROLOGUE · an unnamed voice
PLate September, the day Amos died The river field
written		 An unnamed "I" stands in a wet field and looks at his father, who lies under his tractor with the engine still running. The narrator feels sick, and also relieved, and remembers the threat shouted in front of half the county the night before. An old woman calls "Mr. Hale?" from the porch. The narrator touches nothing and walks back to the car. The voice is Teddy. He only found his father. Lang had killed Amos a few minutes earlier and passed Teddy's car on the lane. Mrs. Pruitt took Teddy for Amos. It is dawn, but the text never says so (draft 4). One of Amos's children killed him, or stood over him and left him to die. It seems to happen in the afternoon, shortly before he was found. The engine is running. One plowed strip. No time of day is given. The narrator never says "I found him" and never says "I killed him". "I leave the engine running. I walk back to the car in my own footprints."
