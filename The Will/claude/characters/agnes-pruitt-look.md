## What the book already says

- "A small woman." The top of her head comes up to June's shoulder.
- "She must be close to eighty, and there's nothing left on her but bones."
- White hair pulled back tight. White eyebrows.
- Pale eyes that don't blink. "Only the eyes aren't old."
- A thin, old voice.
- A gray dress, a white apron, a gray cardigan buttoned up to the neck.
- Swollen knuckles, and hands that shake a little all the time. She holds them in front of her apron, or folded over it.
- A ring of keys in her apron pocket. It clinks when she moves. One of the keys is the only key to Amos's study.
- She climbs the main stairs one step at a time, holding the rail, and stops on the eighth step for breath. She always steps over the ninth step, which cracks. On the cellar stairs she is quick, in the dark, with one hand on the wall.
- Her hearing is as good as her eyes.
- She does not smile.
- Forty years in the house. She cannot drive.

Everything below that is not in this list is a proposal. Change what you like.

## Description

- **Age:** 80.
- **Build:** four foot eleven, very thin and light, narrow shoulders, a slightly bent back. She stands very still.
- **Skin:** pale and papery, with fine lines everywhere and deeper lines beside the mouth. The skin of a woman who has spent forty years indoors.
- **Hair:** pure white, thin, pulled straight back from the forehead into a small tight knot at the back of her head.
- **Face:** small and narrow. High cheekbones, a sharp chin, a thin straight mouth kept closed. White eyebrows.
- **Eyes:** pale gray, clear and steady. They are the youngest thing in her face, and they stay on whatever she is looking at.
- **Hands:** small, with swollen red knuckles and bent fingers. Clean, short nails. The hands tremble slightly.

## Clothes in the house (chapter 6 and after, fall and winter)

- A plain gray wool dress with long sleeves, down to the middle of the calf.
- A white cotton apron, washed and ironed, tied at the waist, with one deep pocket.
- A gray knitted cardigan, mended at one elbow, closed to the top button at the neck.
- Thick gray stockings and flat black lace-up shoes.
- A ring of old iron keys in the apron pocket.

## Effects on the story

- **A weak body and a sharp mind.** She is frail, so nobody thinks she could move a suitcase or walk the house at night. Her memory is exact, and in chapter 31 it is right.
- **The hands.** She cannot lift June's suitcase in chapter 6. In chapter 18 she tells how she got it to the front door.
- **The keys.** June hears them when she walks. The study key is on that ring, and June will have to ask her for it.
- **The ninth step.** She has stepped over it for forty years. On the nights when things are moved in June's room, that step makes no sound.
- **The cellar stairs.** She is quick and sure on them, the one place in the house where she is. So her fall in chapter 33 should feel wrong to the reader.
- **The cardigan buttoned to the neck** is the habit of that house. Amos and Wade button their shirts the same way.
- **The eyes.** In chapter 5 one of them looks June over through the gap of the door. First reading: an old servant pricing a stranger. True reading: she is comparing June with a photograph (chapter 31).
- **The voice.** "Thin and old", the same words in the prologue and in chapter 5.
