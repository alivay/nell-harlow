# Amos Hale: how he looks

Agreed on 12 November 2026. Use this for every image and video of Amos, so that he stays the same man. The reference picture is `Amos_face.png`.

## Description

- **Age:** 81.
- **Build:** once six feet tall, now stooped, with shoulders still wide. Lean and wiry, not fat.
- **Skin:** weathered, reddish-brown and leathery on the face, neck and hands from a lifetime outdoors. Deep creases on the forehead and around the eyes, a cross-hatched neck, freckles and sun spots on the cheeks, temples and backs of the hands. The forehead is paler above the line of his cap.
- **Hair:** thin and white, cropped short, showing at the sides under the cap.
- **Beard:** short, white and rough, cut unevenly by himself, stained yellow around the mouth from the pipe.
- **Eyes:** pale blue and a little cloudy, deep-set under heavy white eyebrows.
- **Glasses:** old rectangular wire-framed glasses, steel-coloured.
- **Face:** a large nose, a thin mouth turned down at the corners, a heavy jaw, large ears. He looks at people as if he suspects them. He never smiles.
- **Hands:** very big, with swollen knuckles and cracked nails.
- **Clothes:** faded blue bib overalls over a grey work shirt buttoned to the neck. Cracked brown lace-up work boots. A faded green cap with a worn, blank yellow patch on the front and a sweat stain.
- **The barn coat:** brown canvas with a corduroy collar. He wears it in flashbacks. On the morning he dies he wears a dark wool vest instead, and the coat stays on its hook in the house. June wears that coat for the whole year, so it must not be on his body.
- **Pipe:** short, straight and dark, with a chewed stem.

Realistic photograph, natural light.

## Effects on the story

- **Chapter 11.** Ruth says June has "his stubborn chin". The beard covers his chin, so the line becomes "his stubborn jaw".
- **Prologue.** He lies under the wheel, so the narrator sees only pieces of him: a hand, the green cap on the ground, perhaps the pipe. He is in the wool vest, not the barn coat.
