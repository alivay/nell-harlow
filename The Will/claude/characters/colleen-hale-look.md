# Colleen: how she looks

Proposed on 16 November 2026. Amos's daughter, Wade's and Teddy's sister. She first appears in chapter 3, in Lang's office. Use this for every image and video of Colleen, so that she stays the same woman.

**The rule for every profile picture (Aviel, 16 November):** the character looks straight at us, the face is very clear, a little of the shoulders shows, and the background is gray.

## Description

- **Age:** 50. She works hard to look 45.
- **Build:** slim, 5'7", straight-backed. She sits very upright.
- **Face:** narrow, with sharp cheekbones, a fine straight nose and thin lips. Her face comes from her mother, not from Amos.
- **Eyes:** gray-green, cool and steady.
- **Hair:** a smooth chin-length bob, colored a warm chestnut brown by an expensive salon, every strand in place.
- **Skin and makeup:** well cared for, with fine lines at the eyes and mouth. Careful, light makeup and a muted rose lipstick.
- **Hands:** slim, manicured, with pale pink polish. The right thumbnail is bitten down to the skin, the only thing about her that is not perfect.
- **Jewelry:** a thin gold watch on her left wrist and small gold earrings.

## Effects on the story

- **She has her mother's face, not her father's.** Wade and June both have Amos's pale blue eyes and his jaw. If every Hale had them, a reader would see June's face in all of them. With Colleen they are just one family's mixed looks.
- **Her hair is perfect, and June's has an inch of roots.** That is why Colleen's eyes stop on June's roots: she notices what she pays to hide.
- **The real things she wears** are bought on debt (chapter 42). The thumbnail is the only sign of it.
