# Julian Cross: how he looks

Agreed on 13 November 2026. He first appears in chapter 2, in Lottie's diner. Use this for every image and video of Julian.

## Description

- **Age and build:** 38, six foot one, lean and well built.
- **Face:** handsome in a quiet way. Short dark blond hair, neatly cut, pushed back. Clean-shaven. Gray-green eyes, calm and steady. Straight nose, defined cheekbones, firm jaw.
- **Expression:** a small, relaxed half-smile. A patient man, quietly amused, who gives nothing away.
- **His one tag:** clean boots. He dresses like a farmer, and nothing he wears has ever been in a field.
- **Clothes in chapter 2:** a brand-new dark green canvas field coat with the store creases still in the sleeves, a charcoal crew-neck sweater, dark jeans, clean tan leather work boots.
- **Hands:** a slim steel watch on his left wrist. No ring.
