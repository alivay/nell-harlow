# Sam Tanner: how he looks and who he is

Proposed on 13 November 2026, updated the same day. He first appears in chapter 1, on the bus. Use this for every image and video of Sam.

## Who he is

- **Ruth Tanner's grandson**, about 35. He was born near Marlow and lives in Indianapolis.
- **Money:** he had some and lost it. His car is gone and so is his watch. He says the car is "in the shop".
- **Why he comes:** every three months he takes the bus to his grandmother's. She owns 160 acres on the river, beside the Hale farm, and he wants to stay in her will. He admits it as a joke.
- **This visit:** Ruth telephoned him on Sunday. Since Amos died she does not want to be alone out there.

## Why he is a suspect

- The data center company wants the whole bend of the river: Ruth's farm and the Hale farm together, or neither.
- Amos refused to sell. So Ruth's land could not be sold while he lived, and she is 89.
- Amos's death frees the sale. Sam needs the money now.
- After the death, the Tanners need the new owner of the Hale farm to sell. That is why Sam is so helpful to June.
- **The truth:** the Tanners gained from the death and had no part in it.
- **Test readers:** two out of two made him their first suspect after chapter 1.

## Description

- **Age and build:** about 35, six feet tall, lean.
- **Face:** good-looking, and he knows it. Dark brown hair a little too long, two days of stubble, warm brown eyes, an easy grin.
- **His one tag:** a pale stripe on his left wrist where a wide watch used to be.
- **Clothes:** a tan waxed-canvas jacket that was expensive five winters ago, frayed at the cuffs. A dark henley shirt, faded jeans, good brown leather boots.
- **He carries:** a canvas duffel bag.
- **Cologne:** a good one. June knows it, because the man who framed her wore the same.

## Personality

- He talks about himself, and he asks too many questions.
- He is charming and easy to read. June handles him the way she handles a customer.
- He is quicker than he acts. He works out for himself that her dead man is Amos Hale. He should not keep doing this in later chapters, or he becomes the author's voice.
- He stops smiling once in chapter 1: when he says his grandmother cannot sell.

## What he knows after chapter 1

- "Nora" is named in Amos Hale's will.
- She says she never met Amos and never heard his name before the letter.
- He believes she must have "done something" for the old man. He thinks she was his woman.
- He tells his grandmother all of it in the car that night.

## Where he appears

- **Chapter 1.** The bus, and the gas station in Marlow.
- **Chapter 3 (written).** He waits at Lang's street door in the morning, uninvited, to walk her in. Lang sends him away, because he is neither family nor named in the will. June decides he is there for the news.
- **Chapter 5 (written).** He watches Lang's door from the window of Lottie's, and then drives June to the farm in Ruth's Buick. He works out from her suitcase that she got the farm, and asks whether she would sell. He tells her how the town knew she was coming, and he names Julian Cross. She sends him away in the yard.
- **Next.** He rests for a few chapters, and comes back at his grandmother's table (chapter 11).

- **Chapter 2.** Julian must be a different kind of charming man. Sam asks too many questions. Julian asks none, and June is relieved. That relief is the clue.
- **Chapter 3.** The children's idea that she was their father's mistress now has a source in the county before the meeting.
