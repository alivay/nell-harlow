# Teddy Hale: how he looks

Proposed on 16 November 2026. Amos's youngest son. He first appears in chapter 3, in Lang's office. Use this for every image and video of Teddy, so that he stays the same man.

**The rule for every profile picture (Aviel, 16 November):** the character looks straight at us, the face is very clear, a little of the shoulders shows, and the background is gray.

## What the book already says

- Ten years younger than Wade: about 44.
- "A soft, friendly face."
- "The only one of them who looks as if he slept last night."
- He leans by the window with his hands in his pockets.
- He gives June a small nod, "the warmest thing in the room".
- He makes the jokes that try to stop the fight. He puts a hand on his sister's shoulder.

Everything below that is not in this list is a proposal. Change what you like.

## The rule for his look

He must look kind, easy and rested, and nothing else. In draft 2 of chapter 3 he had a shaking hand, a red face and a cracking voice, and the test reader guessed at once that he was the voice in the field. His drinking and his debts stay out of every picture until chapter 21.

## Description

- **Age:** 44.
- **Build:** 5'11", medium build, soft around the middle. Smaller and rounder than Wade in every way.
- **Face:** round and boyish for his age, with full cheeks, a short straight nose and a wide mouth that smiles easily. Deep smile lines at the corners of his eyes. He has his mother's face, like Colleen, not his father's.
- **Eyes:** warm hazel, friendly.
- **Hair:** sandy brown, thick, a little too long, pushed back with his fingers and falling forward again.
- **Skin:** lightly tanned, smooth. He works indoors and it shows next to Wade.
- **Shave:** shaved this morning, a little carelessly, with a shadow left along the jaw.

## Clothes at the lawyer's office (chapters 3 to 5, Wednesday 28 October)

- A navy blue sport coat, a good one bought years ago, a little shiny at the elbows.
- A light blue oxford shirt, open at the collar, no tie.
- Tan chinos and brown leather loafers, worn at the heels.
- No coat. He walked over from his flat in town.

## Effects on the story

- **He has his mother's face, like Colleen.** Only Wade has Amos's face. So June's pale blue eyes and her jaw match one brother, and not the whole family.
- **Open collar against Wade's buttoned collar.** The easy brother and the hard one, without a word.
- **He looks rested** because he drinks and has trained himself not to think about that morning (chapters 21 and 50). The picture shows only the rest.
- **Hands in his pockets.** One test reader matched this to the voice in the field ("my hands jammed into my pockets"). The proposed fix in chapter 3, to give him something else to do with his hands, was never made. Decide it. If chapter 3 changes, prompt 2 changes with it.
