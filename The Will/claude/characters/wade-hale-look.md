## Description

- **Age:** 55.
- **Build:** six foot three, heavy and broad. Thick neck, wide sloping shoulders, a deep chest. The body of a man who has lifted things all his life.
- **Skin:** weathered and reddish-brown, one even tone across the face, forehead, neck and hands.
- **Hair:** gray-brown, cropped short, thin on top.
- **Face:** his father's face, thirty years younger. A heavy square jaw, a large nose, large ears, a thin mouth held in a straight hard line. Freshly shaved for the lawyer, with one small razor cut on the jaw.
- **Eyes:** pale blue, deep-set under heavy graying eyebrows. Red at the rims, with dark, swollen shadows under them. He looks worn out, and he looks at people hard.
- **Hands:** very big, thick fingers, scarred knuckles, clean for today.

## Effects on the story

- **He looks like his father.** The jaw, the nose, the ears and the pale blue eyes are Amos's (see `amos-hale-look.md`). So when June sees Amos's photograph in the house, she can know whose father he is at once.
- **The shirt buttoned to the neck** is his father's habit too. Nobody has to say it.
- **The eyes.** He has not slept since he found his father. First reading: guilt. True reading: grief (chapters 15 and 30).
