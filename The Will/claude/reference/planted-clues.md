# The Will: everything planted, and where it pays off

Started on 13 November 2026. One row for every detail the reader has been asked to notice. A detail that never pays off is worse than no detail, so every row needs a chapter in the last column before the book is finished. Add rows after each new chapter. Updated on 15 November 2026 after chapter 3, draft 3, and prologue, draft 4. Chapters 6 and 7 added on 16 November. Checked on 17 November against chapters 1 to 7 as written. The pay-off chapters from 9 to 18 are provisional, because those chapters will be planned again for the locked study.

## Notes from the test readers

- Both readers made Sam their first suspect after chapter 1. He has to come back and be cleared.
- One reader expects the prologue narrator to be felt in the room at the will reading. In chapter 3 the reader should be able to try the prologue voice on each of the three children.
- One reader expects the Hale child who is kind to June to be the killer. That is the reading we want for Teddy.
- One reader guessed that "Nora Bell" was the name of Amos's dead wife. It is wrong, and it is harmless. The name Bell belongs to Dot Bell, the hired girl (chapter 11).
- Kimi K3, after chapter 7 (16 November), put Teddy first and said he was "secretly present in the prologue, while Wade found the body later". Three things led it there: Teddy told the tavern, the little shoes are his, and Mrs. Pruitt lied to the sheriff for somebody. Chapter 8 must pull the reader back to Wade.
